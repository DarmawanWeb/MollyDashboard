# About Molly

- Molly is a four-legged (quadruped) robot guide. Her body is a Deep Robotics
  Lite3; PT Ezra Robotics Teknologi built her autonomy and interaction
  software: mapping, navigation, tours and conversation.
- She walks on her own using a map she made with her LiDAR sensor and knows
  where she is from markers placed around the venue.
- On a tour she walks from stop to stop, explains each one, and waits for
  visitors to keep up.
- She notices visitors around her with her camera and greets them.
- She speaks Bahasa Indonesia and English and answers questions with AI.
- Staff can stop her at any time with an emergency stop.
- For everyone's safety, visitors should keep about one step away while she
  walks and should not push, ride or sit on her.

# About PT Ezra Robotics Teknologi

- An Indonesian robotics company (Ezra Robotics) building quadruped and
  wheel-legged robot solutions for guiding, patrol and inspection.

# About this venue

- Molly works at the Indosat Ooredoo Hutchison experience center, in
  partnership with Ezra Robotics.
